Location / Al Hamraniyah

Minutes from the airport. Down the road from Wynn.

The farm sits ten minutes from Ras Al Khaimah International Airport and half an hour from Wynn Al Marjan Island, the UAE's first integrated resort, opening in 2027. Dubai is 45 to 60 minutes away.

The estate, through your criteria

Thirty to thirty-five keys, at very low density.

Around 22.8 acres of combined landholding — a 395,000 sq ft core estate and 600,000 sq ft of expansion land — support 30 to 35 exceptionally generous keys. Every accommodation type exceeds 800 square feet — the hall suites several times over.
